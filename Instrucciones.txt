Key-Based Enhancement of DES (KE-DES) and the Enigma Machine

In this assignment, students will explore an enhanced version of the Data Encryption Standard (DES) algorithm [1], referred to as KE-DES (Key-based Enhanced DES) [2]. Although KE-DES is not cryptographically secure, its operations are quite similar to those of the original DES algorithm. To complete this task, students should read the description of KE-DES available on the Blackboard website.
